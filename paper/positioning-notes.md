# Positioning the revelation example

Checked 21 October 2026. This is a focused prior-art check, not a systematic novelty clearance.

## Existing results rule out a broad novelty claim

E11, Anshelevich, Das and Naamad, [publisher original](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10458-011-9184-3), already studies cardinal welfare losses from requiring stable matchings and explicitly reports arbitrarily bad loss under general preferences. Its 2013 journal issue followed online publication in 2011 and a 2009 conference predecessor. E10, Boudreau and Knoblauch, [publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0165489613000838) and [opened 2011 working paper](https://www.accessecon.com/pubs/PET11/GeneralPDFPET11/PET11-11-00269S.pdf), formalizes price-of-stability comparisons using welfare functions. Thus neither welfare costs of stability nor unbounded ratios should be called new here. The E11 full paper was not accessible from its old author URL, which redirected to a department home page; the publisher metadata and abstract, not an unverified PDF, support this warning.

E01 studies costly bilateral interviews; E02 studies information-acquisition incentives and regret-free stability; E04 studies low-communication attainment of stable outcomes. They require the draft to distinguish information costs, learning accuracy, attainable stability and cardinal welfare. A construction does not establish that the broader question was previously unstudied.

## A defensible restricted contribution

Frame the result as an explicit finite witness: expanding an inspected graph under **mandatory stable reassignment on all revealed acceptable edges** can select a matching with lower aggregate welfare. If a bounded-utility 2-by-2 parameterized construction makes the full-revelation/restricted-revelation welfare ratio tend to zero, state the normalization, outside options, graph sequence, limiting parameter and positive denominator. Call it an arbitrarily small welfare retention ratio, rather than an additive loss becoming arbitrarily large while all utilities stay bounded. Do not quietly replace the stable matching selected by DA with the best stable matching in defining a price-of-stability statistic.

The old allocation remains feasible for an unconstrained planner on the enlarged graph but can become unstable. This explains the loss through a changed stability restriction. It is not a theorem that an informed unconstrained Bayesian decision maker is worse off. If the planner can discard newly revealed edges, ignore information or choose the previous action under the same objective and costs, its optimized value weakly increases by feasible-set inclusion. That optional-discard comparison is an elementary decision-set argument; a full Blackwell theorem or an endogenous information-design equilibrium is not proved by it. If agents can form deviations outside the discarded graph, discarding a record does not restore full-information stability, so the institutional commitment assumption must be explicit.

## Questions that remain before a research novelty claim

The focused search did not establish whether the exact bounded 2-by-2 graph-revelation construction already appears in prior work. A systematic search of welfare comparative statics under edge addition, stable marriage with incomplete lists, information design and endogenous interviews is still required. Persistent heterogeneity, strategic revelation and manipulation, and endogenous inspection choices remain outside a static graph witness. Do not describe a scheduler heuristic as an optimal or incentive-compatible revelation policy. Distinguish universal counterexamples from average synthetic policy effects, and both from empirical treatment effects.
