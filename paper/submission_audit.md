# Draft audit and scientific limits

This is a research working paper, not a submission-ready article. The following
items record what the project actually completed and what remains unresolved.

## Completed checks

- Full-text organization was studied in three close papers, with versions
  distinguished in `section-models.md` and metadata in `references.json`.
- Finite proofs cover a bounded single-edge graph-expansion witness, the
  three-gap identity, and finite-schedule cost comparative statics. A local lemma
  explains the stable matched-set enumeration. No priority claim is made.
- Independent executable checks cover rational witnesses, exhaustive tiny
  matching sets, rectangular markets, strict tie refinement, exact budgets,
  hidden-information isolation, and planner inequalities.
- Six paired contrasts use new simulation worlds and an internally recorded
  plan. The separate selection diagnostic is explicitly post hoc.
- Numerical macros and tables come from saved results. All six intervals
  can be reconstructed from world-level rows, with sources and outputs hashed.
- The original observed encounter file and codebook were inspected locally.
  Aggregates and sample-composition sensitivity are published; raw participant
  records and third-party full-text articles are not redistributed.
- The PDF is locally compiled and rendered for page-level visual review.
  Exact check outputs and current CI status are reported with the delivered PR.

## Outstanding scientific and author checks

External sources were inspected with AI assistance. An accountable human has
not been recorded as verifying every citation, supporting locator and claim.
The reference and authorship manifests deliberately retain that incomplete
state. Several sources have restricted full-text access; the manuscript limits
associated statements to the publisher record or abstract.

The bounded construction is an explanatory graph-expansion witness. Prior
literature already studies cardinal stability losses, including severe welfare
loss. The package is not a systematic novelty search or a claim of a previously
unknown impossibility theorem. A publishable contribution requires a specialist
assessment against the closest information-acquisition and welfare papers.

The synthetic model has free global scores, fixed inspection budgets, no
individual capacity limits, no endogenous entry, no strategic reporting, and
no belief updates from inspected utilities. A larger Monte Carlo sample does
not validate those structural assumptions. Bootstrap intervals have nominal,
approximate coverage; no finite-sample family-wise guarantee is claimed.

The empirical audit does not identify cardinal utility, a platform policy
effect, or long-term relationships. All-wave and exclusion analyses are not a
replication of the original article's 14-session estimation sample. A real
deployment or participant study would require a separate design and applicable
review; none is performed here.

Authors, affiliations, contribution statements, funding, conflicts, target
venue and submission declarations are unassigned rather than invented. No
submission, publication or distribution to external researchers is undertaken.
